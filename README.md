# Software Engineering Article & Response

[Harness design for long-running application development](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/harness-design-long-running-apps)

**Author:** Prithvi Rajasekaran

**Published Date:** March 24th, 2026

Something I found interesting was how most of the time and money went toward actually building the application rather than planning it. The planner took only 4.7 minutes and cost $0.46, while the first build took over two hours and cost $71.08. The author explains, “Most of the time went to the builder, which ran coherently for over two hours without the sprint decomposition that Opus 4.5 had needed.” This stood out to me because it shows that building something well with AI can still take a lot of time and iteration, while still not being close to perfect. Instead of expecting a finished product from one prompt, the AI was given time to build, get feedback from an agent, and go back to fix what was missing. It shows that with enough time and supervision, AI can be used to create much more complete applications.
